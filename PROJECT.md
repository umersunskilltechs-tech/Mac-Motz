# Project direction

Mac Motz is a fine-art/documentary photographer whose work follows place, transit, family, and quiet spaces. The experience should feel editorial and cinematic rather than like a generic portfolio template.

## Design decisions

- Near-black field, warm paper type, and acid-chartreuse punctuation.
- A large, landscape-driven hero establishes scale before the work.
- Uneven editorial image grid gives each collection a distinct pace.
- Motion enters content as it becomes relevant: a masked hero image, staggered opening copy, restrained in-view artwork reveals, and responsive hover detail.
- Every effect respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
